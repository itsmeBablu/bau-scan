# Design Studio

On iPhone, use the **Studio** main tab or open a House/Room row's **Options (•••) → Studio**. Studio opens directly for that scan, full screen, with floating tools and a pull-up object inspector. Saved detail screens also offer **3D Edit** in Options. The Mac sidebar retains **Design Studio**, with House and Technik Room sections; saved technical surveys offer a Technik Studio action.

## Independent storage

Studio copies the shell and recognised object dimensions into a separate design. Save writes `SceneEdits/<source-kind>-<source-UUID>.json` alongside the library folders, never into `.roomscan`, `.housescan` or `.technicalroom` evidence. There is one saved working design per source. Export the editable JSON project to keep additional variants; importing a variant into the same source is undoable. Designs remain listed even if their source is later removed. JSON records source IDs, scanned size/placement references, chosen families, colours/materials, themes and equipment metadata.

## House Studio

Recognised RoomPlan objects become native parametric models. Available families include sofas, beds, chairs, tables, TVs, cabinets, appliances, bathroom fixtures, radiators and generic boxes/cylinders. Construction uses bounded native shapes and PBR materials; this is an independent native implementation inspired by SmartBau's parametric approach, not a bundled copy of its Three.js renderer or an imported model collection.

Initial models fit the scan bounding dimensions. Sofas vary cushion count, beds adapt pillow count, cabinets vary module count and radiators vary section count. Choosing a different model loads that model's default size (for example, Sofa no longer inherits Bed dimensions). **Fit scanned size** and **Restore scan position** explicitly restore saved references. Appearance remains a generic representation rather than exact identification of a manufacturer's furniture.

Tap/click an object or choose it in the inspector. The active Orbit/Move control and enabled overlay tools are highlighted. Selection picks the whole oriented object envelope, including gaps between visible model parts, and adds a blue rectangular outline with exactly 12 edges, without diagonal triangulation lines. Choose **Move** from the Orbit/Move/Rotate control for red X, green Y and blue Z handles: X/Z move along the floor axes, Y changes elevation; dragging the body moves it across the floor. Axis-constrained snapping preserves the other two coordinates, and rejected collisions roll back the drag. Choose **Rotate** for a horizontal rotation ring; Snap uses 15° steps. Numeric angle entry remains available. Selection and handles are excluded from PNG/model exports. Edit XYZ, dimensions and yaw numerically for precision. Orbit mode retains camera controls. Undo/redo supports design edits. Native forms, menus, file panels and unsaved-change protection are used on both platforms.

On iPhone the scene occupies the entire Studio canvas behind floating controls. The low Models, Move, Edit, Style, Sizes and More dock opens panels or toggles interaction and 3D dimensions; undo and camera reset remain in More. Models has a searchable icon catalogue. Portrait uses a pull-up inspector; landscape keeps the compact dock along the bottom and can show the inspector alongside the scene. The inspector is initially hidden. Controls use system materials and adaptive foreground colours. The House and Technik catalogues are distinct. House replacement uses model defaults; Technik reclassification preserves its survey dimensions and orientation.

House appearance offers Natural, Scandinavian, Modern, Industrial and Pastel palettes, custom wall/floor colours, day/night and summer/winter lighting mood. Rounded shapes, a small repeating floor texture, ambient occlusion and one shadow light aim toward the supplied references, without claiming identical offline-render quality. Season is presentation only. Geometry/texture sizes and object counts are bounded; real iPhone performance still needs profiling.

## Technik Studio

Style offers Captured colours / measured shapes, Equipment shapes, Category colours and Monochrome engineering templates. New technical designs start with Equipment shapes. Generic tank, boiler, pump, manifold, enclosure and radiator parts stay inside the saved outer envelope; pipes and ducts keep their surveyed cross-sections and orientation. Templates are presentation interpretations, not manufacturer identification. Applying a template never changes placement, dimensions, scan references or capacity data. Captured colours restores retained camera samples, with neutral grey where none exist. Category colours distinguish element families only, not medium, service or a standard circuit colour. Legacy projects keep their original measured-envelope appearance until a template is selected. There is no automatic detection or curve-fitting upgrade in these templates.

Only accepted recognition results seed detected pipes/ducts. Manual survey routes and equipment also seed editable reconstructions. Unreviewed detections are not silently promoted. Unknown equipment can be added manually using generic shapes. Hidden routes/connections are never fabricated.

MEP models use sharp measured envelopes without decorative chamfers, artificial fittings or furniture smoothing. Pipe/duct lengths, cross-sections and accepted rectangular-duct roll are retained. Captured colours are preserved when supplied by accepted recognition, labelled as camera-sampled and distinguishable from presets/custom colours; captured colour can be restored after editing. Material identity is not inferred from colour. Equipment without sampled colour uses a labelled neutral preset. Original mesh colours/evidence remain in the survey viewer.

Equipment data supports manufacturer/model, kW, litres, m³/h, bar and required front service clearance. Values require a nameplate, manufacturer or calculation reference and are never inferred from scan size. Linked German planning resources include DIN EN 12828, DIN EN 12831-1, VDI 2050, VDI 2035, VDI 6022 and DVGW drinking-water/W 551 material. The applicable edition, manufacturer instructions, state-specific requirements and utility conditions need project-specific review; no standards text, default capacity, universal installation spacing or compliance certificate is generated.

## Snapping and overlaps

Drag placement can snap to a 0.05 m grid and nearby wall surfaces. Numeric entries are not rounded. New/duplicated objects seek a free location. Moves/resizes reject oriented-envelope intersections with objects/walls and penetration below the floor beyond a 0.002 m numerical tolerance. Touching is allowed. **Allow overlaps** is an explicit override for intentional connections. **Check current floor overlaps** reports up to 20 issues already present in the reconstructed design. These are conservative envelope checks, not raw-mesh clash analysis or verification of manufacturer maintenance zones. Existing scan evidence is not changed to resolve collisions.

Studio wall mitres fill only solid endpoints within 0.025 m, with bounded corner geometry. Openings, larger gaps and cutaway visibility are respected. Original wall measurements and assumed wall thickness remain unchanged.

All scanned walls are visible initially. **Cutaway view · hide front walls** is an explicit display option, off by default; it never removes walls from the project or export. Missing surfaces not present in the original scan are not invented.

Camera framing accounts for the viewport aspect ratio and includes the shell and object extents, so portrait does not clip side walls. Rotation reframes the scene after the native viewport lays out. Returning from 2D reattaches the 3D scene to its replacement viewport. House model replacement also updates an uncustomised model name; **Use furniture default size** resets existing design dimensions without requiring a different model selection.

## Export

- PNG: 1920 × 1440 rendering of the current camera, cutaway and lighting, with selection highlight removed; save using the native file panel.
- JSON: full editable Studio project, restricted on import to the same source ID/type.
- USDZ / OBJ-with-materials ZIP: complete walls and modelled objects of the selected floor. Floors are exported separately because the neutral shell model does not reliably retain their original vertical separation.
- PDF / SVG / DXF: edited 2D design footprints using metres. These are schematic design outputs, not certified engineering drawings. Equipment metadata is retained in the editable project.

Live transparent AR recognition overlays are a proposed next stage, not part of this Studio implementation. Automatic boiler/heater recognition, nameplate OCR, universal German sizing rules and verified engineering accuracy are not claimed. Physical camera/depth/placement validation and device performance profiling remain required.

The latest architecture refinement adapts native wall profiles/mitres and window frames from SmartBau, clips bounded coplanar floor overlap, removes exact duplicate architectural triangles, and offers smaller adjustable wood/tile patterns. Full regression validation passed 262 Mac and 260 iOS tests; the compact portrait/landscape layouts were then rechecked and the final signed iPhone build passed. Physical-device AR and capture validation remain pending.

Studio renders one continuous floor node per storey. Floor coverage is combined with known wall footprints to cover display gaps under walls; shared triangulation edges have no vertical caps. Only outer edges and independently entered slab-thickness steps are capped. Room measurement polygons, areas and raw scan files stay unchanged. This is a design slab, not an inferred structural-floor survey; courtyard gaps without floor/wall evidence are retained.

House Studio Style offers SmartBau-style opening templates: single/double/glazed/sliding doors and fixed/casement/sliding/double-hung windows. Door opening and casement angle are design controls. Frames, casings, leaves, glazing and generic hardware are native shapes fitted to the original opening; they do not identify the real installed product or change the scan. These controls currently apply across the design rather than editing individual opening instances.

Compound-floor correction: Studio now normalizes all floor contours and wall footprints into a native compound path, extrudes the resulting outline and flattens it to one floor object per level. Concave outlines, real holes and separate islands are retained. Additional captured floor regions are stored in the design and recovered for legacy designs from available source scans; existing room contours remain fallback evidence where raw polygons are absent. Source measurement polygons are unchanged. Different independently entered slab depths remain layered within the single floor object. Shared doors/windows are suppressed in display when IDs match, or near-coincident dimensions and orientation match across room records; nearby distinct openings are retained. Raw opening records and wall holes are unchanged.

Legacy doorway correction: overlapping door/window records are now compared within a single room as well as across rooms. The display requires at least 80% projected opening overlap, similar dimensions/elevation, parallel axes and separation consistent with a bounded wall-depth allowance. This handles opposite-facing records with different IDs in continuous saved scans. Separate adjacent doors, distinct parallel walls and other storeys remain independent. Saved opening records are retained; no project reset is required.
